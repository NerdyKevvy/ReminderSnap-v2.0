# One snapshot. Less deadline admin.

Checking course sites again and again? Copying deadlines one by one? **ReminderSnap** reads assignment screenshots and PDFs on your device. Review the details, then create reminders and optional calendar planning blocks.

<div class="steps"><div><b>1. Import</b><span>Choose your assignment screenshot or PDF.</span></div><div><b>2. Check</b><span>Confirm each title, deadline and course.</span></div><div><b>3. Save</b><span>See your deadlines in your chosen workflow.</span></div></div>

<a class="cta" href="https://apps.apple.com/app/id6824731033">View on the App Store</a>

Free. English and Simplified Chinese. Availability varies by platform and storefront.

## Your files stay with you

Recognition works locally. No school-account password, advertising, or screenshot upload to an AI service. Optional calendar connections ask for permission when you choose them. [Read the privacy policy](privacy.html).

## Coming in the 2.1 beta

Your assignment calendar becomes the first page, with course colours, local notifications and private iCloud sync. Take a photo of your laptop screen on iPhone or iPad. Stay in ReminderSnap, connect Apple apps, or optionally sign in to Google Calendar.

**2.1 is currently in testing.** Direct Google Calendar access is currently restricted to the configured test account. It creates a dedicated calendar for your selected assignments; it does not request Gmail or school-account access. Changes made in ReminderSnap update linked entries; changes made in external calendar apps are not automatically imported. [Beta setup and limitations](support.html).

## 少一点重复，清楚每个截止日期

不用反复登录各个课程网站、逐项抄写日期。导入作业截图或 PDF，核对标题和截止日期，再保存到提醒事项及可选日历时间块。

识别在设备本地进行，无需学校密码。2.1 测试版新增作业日历、课程颜色、应用通知、私人 iCloud 同步和拍照导入；可选择留在应用内，或按需连接 Apple 应用、Google 日历。Google 连接暂仅对已配置测试账户开放。
