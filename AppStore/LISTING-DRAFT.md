## English

Name: ReminderSnap

Subtitle: Screenshots to Apple Reminders

Category: Productivity

Description:

Less typing. More doing.

Turn assignment screenshots and PDFs into Apple Reminders and optional Calendar blocks. Import files, review the extracted tasks on the same screen, then save the ones you choose.

- Read images, cropped screenshots and PDFs locally with Apple Vision.
- Review and edit titles, courses, deadlines and alert options before saving.
- Keep the actual deadline in Apple Reminders, with an optional earlier Calendar block to make late-night deadlines easier to see.
- Choose your own reminder list and calendar, or create named destinations during setup.
- Customize deadline notifications and extra Calendar alerts.
- Use English or Simplified Chinese on Mac, iPhone and iPad.
- Get started with a guided setup and a safe sample that never writes real tasks.

No server, advertising, analytics SDK or AI subscription. ReminderSnap does not upload your screenshots or assignment text. Any synchronization of saved tasks is handled by Apple and your chosen account provider.

Always review extracted information before saving. Recognition depends on image clarity and layout. Notification delivery depends on system settings and Focus. Native scheduled-reminder display in Calendar requires macOS 15 / iOS 18 or later; companion Calendar events work independently on supported older systems.
