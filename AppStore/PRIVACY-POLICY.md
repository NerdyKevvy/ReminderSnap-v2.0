# ReminderSnap Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026. Publication draft for the developer's review; not yet hosted publicly.

ReminderSnap reads assignment screenshots and PDFs locally with Apple's Vision framework. It does not upload screenshots, assignment text or reminder/calendar contents to the developer or an AI service. The app contains no advertising, analytics SDK, tracking or developer account system.

## Apple Reminders and Calendar

With your permission, ReminderSnap reads available lists, calendars and existing entries to let you select destinations and avoid duplicates. It writes tasks only when you select and save them. Creating a named list or calendar is a separate explicit action. Any synchronization is handled by Apple and the account provider you selected, under their policies.

## Local storage and control

Recognition text and review tasks are held in memory. Recognized text is cleared after successful saving, Clear All, or quitting. Temporary Photos-picker copies are removed after reading or cancellation. Preferences and identifiers used for duplicate/update management remain locally. Clear All does not delete source files or saved Apple entries. Revoke permissions in system settings; edit/delete saved entries using Reminders or Calendar.

## Support

If you contact us through https://github.com/NerdyKevvy/ReminderSnap/issues, the information you choose to provide is handled by GitHub and used to respond to your request. Public issues are public: do not include private screenshots, credentials or personal assignments. The app does not automatically attach your files to support requests.

## Changes

The published policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. The effective date will identify the latest revision.

# ReminderSnap 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 19 日。此文档为待开发者审核的发布草稿，尚未托管到公开网页。

ReminderSnap 使用 Apple Vision 在设备本地读取作业截图和 PDF，不会将截图、作业文本、提醒事项或日历内容上传给开发者或 AI 服务。应用不包含广告、分析 SDK、追踪或开发者账户系统。

经过你的授权，应用读取可用的列表、日历和已有条目，以选择保存位置并避免重复。只有你选择并保存的任务才会写入，创建列表或日历需单独操作。同步由 Apple 及你选择的账户服务商处理，并适用其隐私政策。

识别文本和待核对任务保留在内存中。成功保存、清空全部或退出应用时会清除识别文本。照片的临时副本在读取或取消后移除。偏好设置和用于管理重复任务的标识符保存在本地。「清空全部」不会删除原始文件或已保存的 Apple 条目。可以在系统设置中撤销权限，并在提醒事项或日历中管理已保存的内容。

如通过 https://github.com/NerdyKevvy/ReminderSnap/issues 联系支持，你主动提供的信息将由 GitHub 处理并用于答复。公开问题中的内容是公开的，请勿提供私人截图、凭据或个人作业内容。应用不会自动将文件附加到支持请求中。

如果应用的数据处理方式发生变化，公开政策将更新，并注明新的生效日期。
